Main Page
Rooster’s Health Store & Wellness Bar, located in Columbia, Illinois, is a family-owned community wellness center that combines a natural grocery store, a supplement shop, and advanced recovery therapies.
The business was established in 1995 and was purchased in 2018 by the current owner, Michelle Swan, following a personal journey with her family’s health.
Core Offerings
* Health & Wellness Bar: The only one of its kind in Monroe County, offering organic remedy teas, frappes, and smoothies.
* Natural Grocery & Produce: A curated selection of organic snacks, local dairy, fresh meats, and plant-based alternatives. They specialize in dairy-free, gluten-free, vegan, and keto-friendly products.
* Supplements & Apothecary: A wide range of responsibly sourced vitamins, minerals, and homeopathic remedies (CBD, essential oils, medicinal teas) for men, women, children, and pets.
* The RooSt (Longevity Center): A specialized facility featuring the SuperHuman Protocol, which uses a three-step sequence of Pulsed Electromagnetic Field (PEMF) therapy, Exercise with Oxygen Therapy (EWOT), and Red Light Therapy to improve cellular health and recovery.
Professional Services
* On-site Nutritionist: Provides personalized advice on diet planning, macro counting, and supplement narrowing.
* Guest Holistic Practitioners: The store hosts specialists like Kaila Taylor, a licensed massage therapist offering services such as Reiki, cupping, lymphatic drainage, and Swedish massage.
* Health & Beauty: Non-toxic toiletries, organic makeup, and chemical-free cleaning essentials.
The business emphasizes a "knowledgeable advice" approach, focusing on independent brands and locally farmed foods to help customers navigate their personal wellness journeys.
